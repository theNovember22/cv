National EV Energy Network Optimizer

24-Hour Joint LP/MILP Multi-Cluster Dispatch & Battery Arbitrage Engine
Engineering Problem

Coordinating 1,200 MW of fast-charging and battery-swapping infrastructure across 5 Indian metro clusters (Delhi NCR, Mumbai MMR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai) under highly volatile Day-Ahead Market (DAM) wholesale energy tariffs and intermittent renewable generation without exceeding feeder capacity.

Architectural Contribution

Formulated a 24-hour joint LP/MILP mathematical dispatch model with 600 MWh distributed Battery Energy Storage (BESS). Integrated dynamic State-of-Charge (SoC) retention boundaries and enforced a strict 5% spinning reserve security lock to safeguard against unexpected regional grid frequency drops.

Technical Approach

Modeled time-varying demand profiles and solved the resulting large-scale linear optimization problem using the SciPy HiGHS simplex/interior-point solver in Python. Automated peak-hour battery discharge to perform price arbitrage against IEX market spikes while reserving solar charging windows.

Measured Outcomes

Exceeded the 95.0% target by delivering 98.6% simulated dispatch reliability, lowered battery cyclic degradation by 7.8% (staying below the strict 8.0% contract ceiling), and achieved zero over-allocation events during peak electricity price regimes.

Reliability-Aware Active Learning

Bayesian Uncertainty Estimation & Selective Human-AI Collaboration
Research Problem

Standard deep neural networks tend to yield overconfident predictions on ambiguous, out-of-distribution medical scans. In high-stakes clinical domains, silent misclassifications can be disastrous, while relying on exhaustive specialist labeling is prohibitively expensive.

Architectural Contribution

Extended the ActiveHAI active learning framework with a 4-stage reliability evaluation methodology. Integrated test-time Monte Carlo Dropout to approximate Bayesian posteriors, allowing the pipeline to distinguish true model ignorance (epistemic uncertainty) from sensor noise (aleatoric uncertainty).

Technical Approach

Evaluated the framework on the Diabetic Retinopathy DR-5 medical imaging benchmark using PyTorch. Generated N=20 stochastic forward passes per candidate sample to extract predictive entropy and mutual information, establishing a dynamic rejection threshold that delegates borderline samples to ophthalmologists.

Measured Outcomes

Demonstrated statistically significant gains in error-detection AUROC and AUPRC over standard maximum softmax probability baselines, cutting clinical annotation burden by selectively querying medical experts only when uncertainty exceeds calibrated bounds.
